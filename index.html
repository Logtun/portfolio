Five years of Managing web3 communities

    Mar 2026 – Oct 2026
    • In part, contributed to the growth of the Axis Hub user base from 3,000+ to 15,000+ users in 3 months.
    • Grew the regional Discord from 200+ to 2,000+ members through weekly community activities.
    • Hosted onboarding sessions twice weekly to educate users about the core product.
    • Hosted a weekly X Space (every Friday) and 10+ local collabs with other web3 communities to onboard new users.
    • Created the regional community page on X and grew it from 0 to 600+ members within 5 months.
    • Built a community points system to track participation, and created over 10 video guides for new users.

  2. Community Moderator / Ambassador

    Puffer Finance

    Dec 2023 – Jun 2026
    • Monitored community platforms for compliance with guidelines and terms of service.
    • Engaged with the community by responding to comments, questions and concerns in a timely and respectful manner.
    • Developed and implemented strategies to increase engagement and participation within the community.
    • Created video content to educate new and existing members about the protocol’s product suite.
    • Collaborated with other team members to ensure a cohesive community strategy.

  3. Community Moderator

    Qubit Wallet

    Jul 2023 – Sep 2023
    • Kept the community active and set the tone of engagement.
    • Built and maintained strong relationships with community members, enhancing the project’s reputation.
    • Kept chat channels on-topic and respectful, stepping in when members broke the guidelines.
    • Guided users through wallet setup and common issues, and warned members about scam and phishing DMs.
    • Collected bug reports and feature feedback from users and passed them on to the team.

  4. Assistant Community Manager

    Revel.xyz

    Jan 2023 – Jun 2024
    • Created engaging, project-centred content to improve engagement in the community.

    • Answered member questions and support tickets, escalating complex issues to the core team.
    • Supported AMAs and community events, and helped manage member roles and verification.

  5. Community Moderator

    Metfx (Watch 2 Earn Protocol)
